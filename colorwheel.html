How to use
  1. Choose a photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP). Images above 10 MP are analysed and exported at 10 MP.
  2. Set the number of ROIs (default 5), then drag a rectangle or click a point for each area. The analysis starts after the last one; "Start again" clears them.
  3. Pick a harmony. The photo updates with the settings applied; "original" shows the untouched photo.
  4. Click a colour's number to anchor the harmony on that colour; click again to release it.
  5. Custom: drag the target points on the wheel (Harmony guide view). Dragging a point of any harmony switches to custom with those points; the custom button brings back your last custom points. The photo updates when you let go. Double-click on the outer ring adds a point (up to 6), double-click on a point removes it.
  6. Export the image, the colour grading instruction or the results.
What the numbers assume
  • Hues are HSV/HSL degrees on an RGB wheel, red at 3 o'clock, counterclockwise.
  • Compound uses sector centres at 0, 30, 150 and 180°: the base, one neighbour on the same side, the complement and one near-complement. Adobe Color describes compound only in words; its exact angles are not published.
  • Harmony fit, score, tolerance and the routing into global HSL, colour grading and local masks are this tool's own rules, not DxO's.
  • The applied view approximates DxO's tools; DxO's channel centres, slider scaling and the sign of the local Hue slider are not verified. Check the result in DxO.
  • Near-grey colours (saturation below 5%) have an unstable hue; they get a small tint instead of a hue rotation.
